About us

We research & exhibit craft
for the present and re-discovery
of the future

Hare & Grouse exhibits and promotes historical and cultural arts and crafts. Our collection reflects a high value placed on craft — contemporary painting hung beside heritage objects, each presented with the research that surrounds it — bringing artists and collectors together around works worth keeping.
